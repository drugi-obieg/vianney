Rules

Each rule says how confident it must be before it flags. Move the slider if a rule flags too much or too little. The percentages are placeholders: no labelled data has calibrated them yet.

Edit the rule text

Rules are plain text. Questions and word lists are drafts; correct them to match what you want filtered.

Break reminder

After you have watched for a while, a card that looks like a recommendation tells you to take a break. Only time a video is actually playing counts, summed over all YouTube tabs, and it never leaves this machine.

Subtitles

Subtitles come from YouTube's own player request, so they are only available once captions are on and the player has loaded them.

On-device AI

Optional. Chrome's built-in model can answer a rule's question when a word list alone cannot decide. Without it, the rules still work from their word lists.

Chrome asks for about 22 GB of free disk space for the model and removes it again when free space falls below 10 GB.

Experimental: pictures and sound Experimental

Uses Chrome's built-in model (Gemini Nano) to look at the thumbnail of every video and to listen to the sound while a video plays. It is slow, it only works where Chrome can run the model with picture and sound input, and it can be wrong in both directions. That is why every part can be switched off, every rule has its own slider, and the lists below always win. Nothing leaves your machine. Off until you switch it on.

Thumbnails

Each picture rule asks the model one question per prompt wording for every thumbnail, so this takes a while. Hiding the picture until it is checked is the only way to never see a flagged one, but a slow model leaves empty frames. Verdicts are remembered for 30 days.

Sound

A window of sound is judged after it has played. The check can therefore only react to what comes next: pause the video, mute it until a window comes back clean, or show a label. The first seconds of anything are always heard. If the model is slower than the sound, windows are skipped and counted.

When it is wrong

Every picture and sound rule is in the list of rules above, marked Experimental. A rule that flags too much: lower its slider, or switch it off. A single wrong verdict: use the buttons in the log, or the lists below.

Allow and block lists

A list always wins over a rule, a model and the sliders. One entry per line. For videos: an id or any YouTube address. For channels: the name as YouTube shows it. If a video is on both lists, it is allowed.

Try a picture or a sound

Choose a picture or an audio file from your computer. It is judged here with the rules above, exactly like a thumbnail or a window of sound, and goes nowhere.
