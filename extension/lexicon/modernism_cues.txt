# Modernism, the doctrine that Pius X condemned in Pascendi dominici gregis (1907): that dogma evolves and changes its meaning, that faith is a feeling that rises from within, that the Church must change with the times. Quoted in rules.md. A choice of the rule author (2026-11-26) that it looks for the doctrine in videos, and that these are the words for it; the wording of each cue is the list author's.
# The words of Pascendi itself that are cues: "vital immanence", "religious sentiment", "evolution of dogma", "the Christ of history and the Christ of faith", "agnosticism", "democratic forms". The Catholic idea of the development of doctrine (Newman, Vincent of Lerins) is not a cue.

A question ("Does doctrine evolve?") is not matched.
