# Communism and socialism: content that teaches, praises or promotes them. The condemnations are the Church's (Syllabus of Errors, section IV; Quod apostolici muneris; Quadragesimo anno 120; Divini Redemptoris 58; Catechism 2425: quoted in rules.md). That a rule looks for them in videos, and every cue below, is a choice of the rule author (2026-11-26).
# 0.95 = a slogan or a flat statement of the doctrine itself ("workers of the world unite", "why communism works"). Guarded: a title that names the Church, a pope, Catholics, martyrs, persecution, victims or a condemnation falls to the weight of the bare word and waits for the model (a Catholic warning, a history of the persecution, Fatima's "errors of Russia" is not propaganda).
# 0.75 = a word of the doctrine that teaching and warning both use (it waits for the on-device model, or for a lower slider). 0.50 = the name of the thing, which news and history use too. 0.15 = ambiguous. Cold War history, documentaries and the martyrs under Communism never reach 0.95.
# Names: only Marx and Engels, the authors of the doctrine ("Das Kapital", "The Communist Manifesto"), and the forms of the doctrine that bear a man's name (Leninism, Maoism, Trotskyism, Stalinism). No cue is a derogatory word, and no private person is named. The socialists whom the Index names are in index_socialists.
