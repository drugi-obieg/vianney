# Spiritism and the calling up of the dead: the doctrine of Allan Kardec, spiritist and spiritualist meetings, mediumship, contact with the dead. Canon 1399, 7 forbids books that teach or recommend "the calling up of spirits and the like"; the Catechism of St. Pius X says of "speaking or writing tables" and of consulting "the souls of the dead by means of spiritism": "All the practices of spiritism are unlawful" (quoted in rules.md). That the rule looks for them in videos, and the cues, are a choice of the rule author (2026-11-26).
# Not repeated here: seances, Ouija, psychics and spirit mediums (divination_cues) and spirit guides (occult_cues). The authors and titles of the Index are in index_spiritists.
