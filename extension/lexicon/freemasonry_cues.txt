# Freemasonry: its lodges, rites, degrees and teaching, and praise of it. A choice of the rule author (2026-11-26, widened the same day), see rules.md.
# The words of Freemasonry itself and nothing else: no conspiracy vocabulary, no names of persons, no derogatory term. Stone masonry ("stone mason", "masonry wall") is not a cue: the bare words "mason" and "masonry" are not in this list.
# The rule flags what presents Freemasonry as good, harmless or true (the rites, the degrees, the teaching, the praise, the invitation to join) and spares what warns against it from the Catholic side (canon 1399, 8: books that present the Masonic and similar sects as useful and harmless are forbidden; books against them are not).
# 0.95 = a lodge, a rite, a degree, a ritual, an invitation to join or a defence of the Craft.
