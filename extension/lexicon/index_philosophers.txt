# The category is a choice of the rule author: the Index is alphabetical and has no categories. Each group below is introduced by the author's heading exactly as the Index prints it ("# @ ..."); tests/index-names.test.js checks every entry against that author's block.
# Ground: canon 1399, classes 2 and 3 (books that defend heresy or try to undermine the foundations of religion; books that of set purpose attack religion). See research/index-of-forbidden-books.md and rules.md.
# The rule looks for videos ABOUT these authors and their works (an analysis, a summary, a reading, an adaptation, a biography, a debate), whatever the video says of them.
# 0.95 = the full name or a title that only this author wrote; 0.75 = a bare surname that a place, a school or a pet may also bear (it waits for the on-device model); 0.50 = a broader or ambiguous bare word.
# A name at 0.95 is a pattern that stands down to the plain 0.75 line under it (the model decides) when a word of a street, a school, a hotel, a station or the like stands next to it (Rue Voltaire, Hotel Victor Hugo): the guard is looked at only after the name has matched.
# No title is translated: a title appears only as the Index prints it. A name or a title that is also a place, a cathedral or an everyday word is not a cue (see rules.md).
